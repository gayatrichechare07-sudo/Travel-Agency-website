About TravelX Agency

TravelX Agency is a newly started travel company created with the dream of making travel simple, affordable, and enjoyable for everyone. Our company was established in 2026 with a small but passionate team who love exploring new places and helping others experience the joy of travel.

🚀 Our Startup

TravelX began as a startup travel agency focused on providing budget-friendly and comfortable travel packages for families, friends, couples, and solo travelers. Even though we are a new company, we are dedicated to offering high-quality travel services and memorable experiences.
